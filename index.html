Novels

Choose one full‑length import for the month.

Novel
Ring
Kōji Suzuki (1991, tr. 2003)
~286 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.8★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: The techno-paranoid Japanese cursed-videotape novel that started it all, colder and more procedural than the films it spawned.

A journalist investigating the deaths of four teenagers who all watched the same mysterious videotape one week before dying races against a self-imposed deadline to break the tape's curse. What starts as a detective story about a viral urban legend turns into a bleak reckoning with a vengeful psychic ghost and the biological, almost virus-like logic of how curses spread.

Origin: Japan

Content warnings: Rape and sexual assault (including a graphic backstory), misogyny, death of children, body horror, smallpox/disease imagery, medical trauma.

Novel
Piercing
Ryū Murakami (1994, tr. 2007)
~192 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.7★
Theme fit
4 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A short, claustrophobic Tokyo psychological horror about two damaged people converging on the same night for very different reasons.

Kawashima Masayuki, a new father haunted by an urge to stab his infant daughter, decides to redirect that compulsion by hiring a call girl he plans to attack instead. What he doesn't know is that Chiaki, the woman he hires, is carrying her own history of abuse and has plans of her own, and the night spirals into an increasingly unstable two-hander.

Origin: Japan

Content warnings: Graphic violence, self-harm, child abuse (referenced), domestic/childhood abuse, sexual content, suicidal ideation, body horror.

Novel
Let the Right One In
John Ajvide Lindqvist (2004, tr. 2007)
~480 pages Debut novel: Yes Avg rating: 4.0★
Theme fit
4 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A grim, wintry Swedish suburb vampire novel that's as much about bullied-kid loneliness as it is about monsters.

Oskar, a bullied twelve-year-old in a bleak Stockholm suburb, befriends Eli, the strange new child next door who only comes out at night — right as a string of brutal murders starts up nearby. Their friendship, tender and unsettling in equal measure, unfolds alongside a much colder, uglier story about the adults around them and what Eli actually is.

Origin: Sweden

Content warnings: Child abuse, pedophilia (a major adult character), graphic violence, animal death, self-harm, body horror, bullying.

Novel
Our Share of Night
Mariana Enríquez (2019, tr. 2022)
~608 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.1★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A sprawling, decades-spanning Argentine horror epic about a father and son trapped inside a monstrous, occult family cult.

Juan, a medium capable of channeling something unspeakable that lives behind reality, tries to protect his young son Gaspar from the wealthy, murderous occult society that has used their family as living conduits for generations. Set against the backdrop of Argentina's dictatorship-era violence, it moves between road trip, family drama, and cosmic/body horror across a huge timeline.

Origin: Argentina

Content warnings: Graphic violence, torture, child endangerment, body horror, animal sacrifice, political violence/dictatorship-era atrocities, death of parents, occult ritual abuse.

Short story
The Secret Life of Insects
Bernardo Esquinca (tr. 2021)
~15 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: N/A
Theme fit
4 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A quiet, dread-soaked Mexican mystery about a forensic entomologist confronting a murder that shouldn't be physically possible.

A forensic entomologist investigating his wife's death is confronted with an impossible detail: evidence suggests she died in a forest at the same moment she appeared to be sleeping beside him in bed. As he pulls at the thread using his own scientific specialty, the story tips from procedural mystery into something stranger and more unsettling.

Origin: Mexico

Content warnings: Death of a spouse, body horror (insect/decomposition imagery), grief, murder.

Short story
The Aleph
Jorge Luis Borges (1945)
~20 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: N/A
Theme fit
3 / 5
CW Mild

Vibe: Less a scare than a slow vertigo — an Argentine literary classic about staring directly at the incomprehensible size of everything.

A grieving writer, still visiting the family of his dead beloved out of habit, is led into a rival poet's basement to witness "the Aleph": a small point in space containing every point in the universe, seen all at once. The story leans more cosmic and philosophical than gory, but the disorientation and cosmic-horror-adjacent scale of what the narrator sees (including glimpses of decay) fit an unsettling, mind-bending theme.

Origin: Argentina

Content warnings: Existential/cosmic dread, grief, brief unsettling body imagery.

Short story
The Human Chair
Edogawa Ranpo (1925)
~20 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: N/A
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A deeply uncomfortable Japanese ero-guro classic about obsession, hiding in plain sight, and the horror of being an object.

A young author receives a letter-confession from a self-described "ugly" craftsman who built a hollow armchair he could hide inside, letting him press against — and become obsessed with — the strangers, mostly women, who sat on it without knowing he was there. The story escalates his voyeuristic fixation until it curdles into something far more disturbing, then twists the reader's own comfort at the end.

Origin: Japan

Content warnings: Non-consensual voyeurism/stalking, sexual undertones, psychological horror, themes of obsession and dehumanization.

Short story
The Queen of Spades
Alexander Pushkin (1834)
~30 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: N/A
Theme fit
4 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A Russian gothic classic about greed, a ghost, and a card trick that costs a man his sanity.

An ambitious officer named Hermann becomes obsessed with an elderly countess rumored to know the secret to winning at cards, and manipulates her ward to get close to her. When his scheme goes fatally wrong, the countess's ghost delivers the winning numbers from beyond the grave — with a catch that unravels Hermann's mind by the final hand.

Origin: Russia

Content warnings: Death (elderly character frightened to death), manipulation/coercion, gambling addiction, descent into madness/institutionalization.
