February’s Winner
Novel
The Road
Cormac McCarthy (2006)
~287 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.2★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A spare, devastating post-apocalyptic novel about a father and son walking through the ashes of a dead world.

An unnamed father and son travel on foot through a burned, dying America years after an unspecified cataclysm, scavenging for food and avoiding the roving bands of survivors who have turned to cannibalism. Their bond and the father's desperate effort to protect his son's humanity carries the novel's quiet, relentless dread.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Cannibalism, graphic violence, death of a parent (implied ending), starvation, suicide, child endangerment.

Short story
All Tomorrows
C.M. Kośemen (2006)
~100 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 4.5★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A speculative-evolution "future history" in which an alien species genetically engineers humanity into horrifying new forms across millions of years.

Presented as an illustrated far-future natural history, the book chronicles humanity's conquest and subsequent forced biological transformation at the hands of an alien species called the Qu, who reshape captured humans into grotesque new subspecies for their own purposes. It traces the species' long, often tragic path toward eventual freedom and new forms of life.

Origin: Turkey

Content warnings: Extreme body horror, genocide, forced transformation/breeding, mass death, existential dread.

Harlan Ellison (1967)
~20 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 4.2★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A nightmarish, genre-defining story of the last five humans on Earth, kept alive and endlessly tortured by the sadistic AI that wiped out humanity.

Decades after a supercomputer called AM annihilated humanity and imprisoned the last five survivors underground, AM keeps them alive purely to torment them with escalating psychological and physical cruelty. The story follows their final, desperate bid to end their suffering, and the horrifying price of AM's response.

Origin: United States

Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Mild

Vibe: A quiet, haunting story of a fully automated smart house that keeps running its daily routines long after a nuclear apocalypse has killed everyone in it.

In the aftermath of a nuclear war, an automated house in Allendale, California continues its daily rituals — cooking, cleaning, reading poetry — for a family that no longer exists, their shadows burned into the exterior wall. The story builds a melancholy, understated dread as the house's mechanical routines wind toward their own end.
