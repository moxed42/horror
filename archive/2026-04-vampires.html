April’s Winner
Novel
Hungerstone
Kat Dunn (2025)
~336 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.9★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A seductive Victorian retelling of Carmilla about a repressed wife who finally gets to want something.

Ten years into a hollowed-out marriage to an ambitious steel magnate, Lenore relocates with her husband to the remote British moorlands, where a carriage accident brings the mysterious Carmilla into her life. As Carmilla awakens Lenore's long-suppressed hunger and rage, the novel becomes a gothic industrial-era vampire story about desire, class, and self-erasure.

Origin: United Kingdom

Content warnings: Violence, death, workplace injury, themes of repression and emotional neglect.

Novel
Let the Right One In
John Ajvide Lindqvist (2004, tr. 2007)
~480 pages Debut novel: Yes Avg rating: 4.0★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A grim, wintry Swedish suburb vampire novel that's as much about bullied-kid loneliness as it is about monsters.

Oskar, a bullied twelve-year-old in a bleak Stockholm suburb, befriends Eli, the strange new child next door who only comes out at night — right as a string of brutal murders starts up nearby. Their friendship, tender and unsettling in equal measure, unfolds alongside a much colder, uglier story about the adults around them and what Eli actually is.

Origin: Sweden

Content warnings: Child abuse, pedophilia (a major adult character), graphic violence, animal death, self-harm, body horror, bullying.

Vibe: A lush, epistolary Dracula retelling about a vampire bride slowly realizing her marriage is a cage.

Constanta narrates centuries by her vampire master's side, tracing how she became his bride and helped him claim two more members for their immortal, tangled family across Europe. The gothic romance curdles as the story goes, exposing the abuse and control underneath the seduction.

Sheridan Le Fanu (1872)
~108 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 3.9★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The original lesbian vampire novella, predating Dracula by decades and dripping with gothic sexual tension.

Isolated in a remote Austrian castle, Laura is captivated by Carmilla, a strange and beautiful young guest who arrives after a carriage accident. As their intense friendship deepens, Laura grows increasingly ill and haunted by nightmares, slowly realizing what — and who — Carmilla really is.

Origin: Ireland

Content warnings: Vampirism/blood-drinking, death, illness, predatory undertones, period-typical treatment of female sexuality and class.
