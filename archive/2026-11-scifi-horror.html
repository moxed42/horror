Vibe: Cosmic-dread puzzle box with a surprisingly aching human heart.

Inside a secret organization, a small team fights entities that erase themselves from memory, so knowledge of them vanishes almost as soon as it's learned, along with colleagues, loved ones, and even their own goals. Novel
Blood Music
Greg Bear (1985)
~270 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.8★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: Big-idea biological horror that goes from lab accident to world-changing in a hurry.

A rogue biotech researcher, fired for exceeding his authorization, injects himself with intelligent cells he engineered in the lab to save his work. The cells begin to change him, and then everything around him. Expanded from Bear's award-winning 1983 novelette, it's a landmark of body horror and sci-fi.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Body horror, loss of bodily autonomy, mass death, apocalyptic themes.

Novel
The Body Snatchers
Jack Finney (1955)
~220 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.9★
Theme fit
4 / 5
CW Mild

Vibe: Paranoid small-town invasion thriller, the origin of 'they're not who they seem.'

In a quiet California town, Dr. Miles Bennell's patients start insisting that their loved ones have been replaced by identical impostors. As he digs in, Miles discovers that something has arrived in town, and that it's spreading. The novel behind four film adaptations (including 1956's Invasion of the Body Snatchers).

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Violence and death, paranoia, loss of identity, period-typical sexism.

Vibe: A cold, awe-struck speculative-evolution chronicle of what humanity could become.

An illustrated, billion-year chronicle of the strange and often grim descendants of humankind, told in the voice of a detached narrator. Originally released as a free online PDF, it became an internet cult classic before getting its first print edition.
