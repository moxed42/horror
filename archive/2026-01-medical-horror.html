Medical student Susan Wheeler grows suspicious after a routine surgery leaves a patient in an unexplained coma, and her investigation turns up a disturbing pattern of similar cases at her hospital. Novel
The Hot Zone
Richard Preston (1994)
~352 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.2★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: The nonfiction Ebola outbreak book so viscerally written it reads like horror fiction.

A true account of the emergence of Ebola and Marburg viruses in Africa, and the terrifying 1989 outbreak of a related filovirus among lab monkeys just outside Washington, D.C. Preston renders the biology and human toll of hemorrhagic fever in unflinching, stomach-churning detail.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Graphic descriptions of illness, bodily fluids, and death; animal death; medical/biohazard horror.

Vibe: A sci-fi horror short about teleportation, anesthesia, and what happens to a mind left conscious across the void.

A father explains the family-friendly history of "the Jaunt," a teleportation technology that requires travelers to be sedated before use, to his children before their first trip. Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: The pulpy, episodic mad-scientist novella that inspired Re-Animator — a medical student obsessed with reviving the dead.

Herbert West, a brilliant and morally unmoored medical student, spends years perfecting a reagent that can reanimate dead tissue, with escalating and increasingly monstrous results. Told by his horrified assistant across a series of gruesome experiments, the story follows West's relentless pursuit of his research at any cost.
