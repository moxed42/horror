November’s Winner
Novel
Into the Drowning Deep
Mira Grant (2017)
~440 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.0★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A big, gory monster-movie novel about the crew sent to find out what really killed the ship that vanished making a mermaid mockumentary.

Seven years after the Atargatis disappeared while filming a fake documentary about mermaids, a new, better-funded expedition sets out to find out what actually happened — bringing scientists, hunters, and a woman searching for her lost sister. What they find in the Mariana Trench is very real, very hungry, and not remotely like the CGI mermaids from the original broadcast.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Extreme gore and body horror, multiple graphic deaths, gun violence, animal death, an abusive ex-partner subplot.

Novel
The Fisherman
John Langan (2016)
~266 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.9★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A cosmic-horror novel about grief and fishing that turns into something much stranger once it reaches the reservoir.

Two widowers, Abe and Dan, bond over fishing trips as a way of coping with the deaths of their wives, eventually venturing to a mysterious stream rumored among locals to be dangerous. An embedded tale about the historic construction of the Ashokan Reservoir unspools alongside their story, both converging on something ancient and cosmic beneath the water.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Graphic violence, child death, sexual content, emotional abuse, suicide, substance use, mental illness, racism (period-typical).

The Upper Berth
F. Marion Crawford (1886)
~30 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 4.0★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A classic maritime ghost story about a cursed berth on an ocean liner that no passenger survives sleeping in.

A seasoned traveler books passage on a transatlantic steamer and is assigned an upper berth in a cabin with an inexplicably damp, cold porthole that won't stay shut. As previous occupants' fates come to light, the story builds toward a confrontation with whatever haunts the cabin at sea.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Death, drowning (implied), supernatural threat/confrontation, period-typical seafaring peril.

November’s Winner
Short story
The Shadow over Innsmouth
H.P. Lovecraft (1931)
~68 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 4.1★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The foundational Deep Ones novella — a decaying New England seaport hiding an ancient bargain with something amphibious.

A young antiquarian's research trip leads him to Innsmouth, a decrepit fishing town whose residents have a strange, fish-like appearance and a deep-seated hostility to outsiders. Trapped overnight, he uncovers the town's generations-old pact with an undersea civilization — and what that means for his own bloodline.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Xenophobic and racially-coded "degeneration" themes (a well-documented issue in Lovecraft's work), body horror, mob violence, pursuit/chase horror.
