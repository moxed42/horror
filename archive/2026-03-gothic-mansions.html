Novels

Choose one full‑length pick for the month.

Novel
Rebecca
Daphne du Maurier (1938)
~449 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.3★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The classic gothic romance-thriller about a new bride haunted by the shadow of her husband's dead first wife — and the great house that seems to still belong to her.

A shy young woman marries the wealthy widower Maxim de Winter and moves into his grand estate, Manderley, only to find herself living in the constant shadow of his glamorous first wife, Rebecca. As the sinister housekeeper Mrs. Danvers keeps Rebecca's memory alive, the new Mrs. de Winter uncovers dark secrets behind Rebecca's death.

Origin: United Kingdom

Content warnings: Death, psychological manipulation and gaslighting, domestic abuse themes, suicide (referenced), fire.

Novel
The Turn of the Screw
Henry James (1898)
~120 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.5★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The ambiguous, endlessly-debated ghost story of a governess convinced malevolent spirits are stalking the children in her care at a remote English estate.

A young governess takes a position at an isolated country house, caring for two children who seem increasingly under the influence of the ghosts of two former servants. As she becomes convinced the children are in supernatural danger, the story leaves deliberately unclear whether the haunting is real or a symptom of her own unraveling.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Death of children (implied/ambiguous), psychological horror, child endangerment, ambiguous supernatural threat.

March’s Winner
Novel
Mexican Gothic
Silvia Moreno-Garcia (2020)
~301 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.7★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Extreme

Vibe: A lush, fungal gothic horror novel about a decaying English manor transplanted to the Mexican countryside, and the rot festering beneath its family's history.

Noemí travels to the remote, decaying mansion High Place to check on her newly married cousin, whose unsettling letters hint that something is deeply wrong with her new husband's family. As Noemí digs into the house's history of colonialism, eugenics, and rot, she uncovers a body-horror secret literally growing in the walls.

Origin: Mexico

Content warnings: Body horror, sexual assault (attempted), incest themes, colonial/eugenics themes, gore, drug use (non-consensual).

Novel
The Haunting of Hill House
Shirley Jackson (1959)
~182 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 3.8★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The gothic haunted-house novel every other haunted-house novel is quietly arguing with, built on dread rather than gore.

Four seekers, including a scholar of the paranormal and the fragile, lonely Eleanor, take up residence in Hill House to study its reputed hauntings. The house's influence grows increasingly personal and psychological rather than overtly supernatural, tightening around Eleanor in particular as the group's grip on reality frays.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Suicide (by hanging and by car collision), suicidal ideation, mental illness/psychosis, gaslighting, death of a parent (off-page).

March’s Winner
Short story
The Fall of the House of Usher
Edgar Allan Poe (1839)
~20 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 3.9★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: The gothic short story that defined the crumbling-mansion genre — a decaying house, a doomed family line, and a premature burial.

A visitor arrives at the ancestral home of his old friend Roderick Usher, finding both the man and his house in advanced states of decay, seemingly bound to each other's decline. As Roderick's twin sister Madeline succumbs to a mysterious illness, the family's collapse and the house's collapse converge in a single catastrophic night.

Origin: United States

Content warnings: Death, premature burial, mental illness/psychological horror, familial decline, implied incestuous undertones.

Vibe: A classic haunted-house short from the author of Dracula — a student's isolated study retreat in a shunned old mansion goes very wrong.

Seeking peace and quiet to study for exams, Malcolm Malcolmson rents a long-vacant, ill-reputed old house once owned by a cruel hanging judge, ignoring the locals' warnings.
