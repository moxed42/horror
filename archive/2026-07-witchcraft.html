Slewfoot: A Tale of Bewitchery
Brom (2021)
~512 pages Debut novel: No Avg rating: 4.3★
Theme fit

CW Extreme

Vibe: A lush, illustrated Puritan-era horror novel about a widow accused of witchcraft who forms a bargain with an ancient forest spirit.

In 1666 colonial Connecticut, newly widowed Abitha is targeted by her suspicious, hostile community right as a strange, ancient being awakens in the nearby woods and offers her his power. As Abitha embraces witchcraft to survive, the story builds toward a bloody reckoning between the Puritan settlement and the older, wilder forces it displaced.

The White People
Arthur Machen (1904)
~40 pages Debut book: No Avg rating: 3.8★
Theme fit
5 / 5
CW Heavy

Vibe: A hallucinatory, dreamlike weird-fiction classic told through a young girl's increasingly disturbing secret diary of witchcraft and hidden rites.

Framed by a philosophical discussion of true evil, the story presents the diary of a young girl who is gradually initiated into a secret, ancient tradition of witchcraft and folklore by her nurse. The diary's dreamlike, fragmented prose builds an atmosphere of creeping wrongness rather than explicit horror.
